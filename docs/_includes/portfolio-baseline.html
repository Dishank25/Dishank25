About me

From screens to search.

I started with datasets and Flutter screens. Working on both gave me a habit I still use. I follow an input all the way to the person who gets the result. In a support assistant, that means opening the source FAQ behind a reply. In a recommendation engine, it means checking why a product reached the top.

I’m based in Noida. My engineering work spans search, document extraction, recommendations, and computer vision. I currently work in AI consulting, bringing those engineering habits to conversations with business users.
