Student & Recruiter Job Portal

A two-sided Flutter app for browsing openings, posting jobs or internships, and reviewing applications.

A student can move from search to an opening and start an application. A separate recruiter flow collects the role, skills, and screening questions for a job or internship posting. Recruiters can also open a list of applicants to review.

This repository hosts a shared app snapshot originally committed by an109. Its dependency configuration connects the screens to repositories, use cases, and BLoCs. Listings and applications need access to the configured backend.

Flutter · Dart · BLoC · REST APIs
